# oPenEfficiency Features

This document provides a comprehensive list of all features, their descriptions, use cases, and how to use them.

## Alignment

### Align Bottom
**Description/Use Case:** Multi-select: aligns bottom edges to the anchor object. Single select: snaps bottom edge to the nearest horizontal guide line (or slide edge). Right-click for 'Move Until Object'.

**How to Use:**
> ### Align Bottom
> Aligns the bottom edges of all selected shapes to the bottom edge of the first shape in the selection.

### Align Center
**Description/Use Case:** Centers all selected objects horizontally relative to the anchor object's center point. With single selection, centers on slide.

**How to Use:**
> ### Align Center
> Centers all selected shapes horizontally relative to the horizontal midpoint of the first shape.

### Align Center Vertical
**Description/Use Case:** Centers objects vertically relative to the anchor object's center point. With single selection, centers vertically on slide.

**How to Use:**
> ### Align Center Vertical
> Centers all selected shapes vertically relative to the vertical midpoint of the first shape.

### Align Left
**Description/Use Case:** Multi-select: aligns left edges to the anchor object. Single select: snaps left edge to the nearest vertical guide line (or slide edge). Right-click for 'Move Until Object'.

**How to Use:**
> ### Align Left
> Aligns the left edges of all selected shapes to the left edge of the first shape. Ideal for snapping columns into a clean left margin.

### Align Right
**Description/Use Case:** Multi-select: aligns right edges to the anchor object. Single select: snaps right edge to the nearest vertical guide line (or slide edge). Right-click for 'Move Until Object'.

**How to Use:**
> ### Align Right
> Aligns the right edges of all selected shapes to the right edge of the first shape.

### Align Top
**Description/Use Case:** Multi-select: aligns top edges to the anchor object. Single select: snaps top edge to the nearest horizontal guide line (or slide edge). Right-click for 'Move Until Object'.

**How to Use:**
> ### Align Top
> Aligns the top edges of all selected shapes to the top edge of the first shape.

### Align to Cell
**Description/Use Case:** Opens dialog to align shapes to their containing table cell.

**How to Use:**
> ### Align to Cell
> Aligns the selected shapes to be perfectly centered inside the underlying table cell directly beneath them. Detects the intersecting table automatically.

### Arrange Grid
**Description/Use Case:** Arranges selected shapes in a grid layout.

**How to Use:**
> ### Arrange Grid
> Arranges all selected shapes into an evenly-spaced grid, calculating an optimal number of rows and columns based on the selection count.

### Arrange Pro
**Description/Use Case:** Opens the Arrange Pro dialog for advanced shape arrangement options.

**How to Use:**
> ### Arrange Pro

**How to Use:**

>

### Diagonal Align

**How to Use:** Select required shapes and execute the feature.

### Distribute Horizontally

**How to Use:** Select required shapes and execute the feature.

### Distribute Vertically
**Description/Use Case:** Distributes selected shapes evenly vertically. Right-click to toggle between 'Align to Slide' or 'Align Selected Objects'.

**How to Use:** Select required shapes and execute the feature.

## Shapes

### Bring Forward
**Description/Use Case:** Brings the selected shape forward one layer.

**How to Use:** Select required shapes and execute the feature.

### Bring to Front
**Description/Use Case:** Brings the selected shape to the absolute front layer.

**How to Use:** Select required shapes and execute the feature.

### Create Motion Path
**Description/Use Case:** Generates an automatic animation. The first selected shape will move precisely to the X/Y coordinates of the second selected shape.

**How to Use:**
> ### Create Motion Path
> 
> Select exactly two shapes. This feature injects a PowerPoint animation path so the first shape automatically travels to the exact coordinates of the second shape when the slide is presented.

### Match Shape Geometry
**Description/Use Case:** Transfers exact vertex angles, shaft thicknesses, and corner radii across similar shapes.

**How to Use:**
> ### Match Shape Geometry
> 
> PowerPoint warps shapes when you resize them — arrowheads become too big, pentagon tips become inconsistent, and rounded corners look different depending on size.
> 
> **How it works:**
> Select multiple shapes of the same type. The **first shape** in the selection is the Master. All other shapes will have their hidden `Adjustments` properties overwritten to match the Master's exact geometry — without changing their size or position.
> 
> **Supported shapes:**
> - Block Arrows (normalizes shaft thickness and arrowhead size)
> - Chevrons & Pentagons (tip angle synchronization)
> - Rounded Rectangles (corner radius equalization)
> - Speech Bubbles / Callouts (tail width and angle)
> - Donut / Arc shapes (inner radius depth)
> 
> *Note: Shapes must be the same AutoShapeType. Mixing an Arrow with a Pentagon will be skipped automatically.*

### Optimize Free Form
**Description/Use Case:** Traverses the path vertices of hand-drawn/freeform shapes and removes mathematical outliers and redundant points to smooth the path globally.

**How to Use:**
> ### Optimize Free Form
> 
> Reduces the number of vertex nodes in a freehand vector path without visually distorting the shape. Useful for cleaning up imported SVGs or hand-drawn paths.

### Send Backward
**Description/Use Case:** Sends the selected shape backward one layer.

**How to Use:** Select required shapes and execute the feature.

### Send to Back
**Description/Use Case:** Sends the selected shape to the absolute back layer.

**How to Use:** Select required shapes and execute the feature.

### Shape Transformation / Vertex Align
**Description/Use Case:** Reads the exact proportions (like shaft thickness vs. head width of a Block Arrow or Pentagon) of a Master shape and forces all other selected shapes to adopt identical vertex ratios.

**How to Use:**
> ### Shape Transformation / Vertex Align
> 
> Transfers vertex-level adjustment values (yellow diamond handles) from the first shape to all other selected shapes of the same type, normalizing arrowhead sizes, corner radii, and tip angles.

### Smart Corners
**Description/Use Case:** Toggles rounded corners on selected rectangle shapes.

**How to Use:**
> ### Smart Corners
> Applies a mathematically uniform corner radius to all selected shapes with a configurable slider. Works independently of the native Format Shape dialog.
