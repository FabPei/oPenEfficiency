# Usage

Once oPenEfficiency is installed, it seamlessly integrates into Microsoft PowerPoint.

## Getting Started

1. **Locate the Ribbon:**
   Open PowerPoint and navigate to the custom **oPen Efficiency** tab on the main ribbon.

2. **Select Elements:**
   Most features require you to select one or more shapes, text boxes, or table cells on your slide. The order of selection often matters (e.g., for "Align to First" tools).

3. **Execute Features:**
   Click a feature in the ribbon or the sidebar to execute it.

## Sidebar & UI

oPenEfficiency is designed to be highly personalizable. The Sidebar serves as your primary productivity hub and can be tailored to match your specific workflow.

- **Toggle Sidebar:** Use the `Ctrl+Alt+S` shortcut to quickly show or hide the sidebar.
- **Settings:** Click the **Gear icon** to access the Settings Window and customize your experience.

### Keyboard Shortcuts
Default shortcuts (Customizable in Settings):
- `Ctrl+Alt+S` - Toggle Sidebar
- `Ctrl+Alt+M` - Match Size
- `Ctrl+Alt+A` - Align to First
